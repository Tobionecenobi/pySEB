Data provenance for the linked-titin tutorial and application
===========================================================

Primary bundle: 1TIT, regenerated 9 November 2026.

SAXS: SASDKC2-titin.csv contains every fourth deposited observation through
q = 0.30 inverse angstrom, with intensities and uncertainties unchanged.
Source: https://www.sasbdb.org/data/SASDKC2/
Cite Joshi et al. (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.bbrc.2021.02.114, and SASBDB.
Reuse with original-author attribution is documented by SASBDB:
https://www.sasbdb.org/aboutSASBDB/

Primary domain cloud: 1TIT-heavy.pdb
Source: https://files.rcsb.org/download/1TIT.pdb
Entry: https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1TIT
Cite Improta, Politou and Pastore (1996), Structure 4, 323-337,
DOI 10.1016/S0969-2126(96)00036-6, and PDB 1TIT.
PDB archive data are CC0: https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
The extract retains all 688 heavy-atom coordinates and original serials
from the single deposited coordinate model; hydrogen records are omitted.
The 89 coordinate residues differ from the experimental domain by F21L,
T42V and A78T. Substituted side chains have not been rebuilt. Nine additional
SEQRES residues lack coordinates and are not added to the cloud. References
are terminal C-alpha atoms 2 and 1358; OXT is retained for comparison with
the historical calculation. Experimental terminal chains are modelled separately.

The original paper's supplementary methods identify 1TIT as the template for
10,000 RanCH conformations per doublet, with GAJOE subensemble selection
(EOM, ATSAS 2.8). Our calculation uses statistical composition with freely
averaged attachment orientations, not EOM conformer selection.

Results: Examples/generate_linked_titin.py executes the tagged calculation
cells in the four titin tutorial pages. The manifest records calculation,
input and output hashes and software versions. Atomic scenes use two copies
of the single 1TIT coordinate model. Gaussian-chain realizations are
illustrative, not experimentally resolved conformations. Hydration sites
represent effective scattering contributions, not individual water molecules.

Historical comparison: 1TIU-heavy-ensemble.pdb retains models 1, 7, 13 and 19
from the 1TIU NMR ensemble, with hydrogens removed. Cite PDB 1TIU and the same
Improta et al. article. Its previous bundles are archived separately as
1tiu-archived-bundle/ (article) and titin-results-1tiu/ (tutorial).
